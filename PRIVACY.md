# Indium Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Indium doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## Your notes

Your notes are Markdown files in folders you choose. Indium reads and writes them only on your Mac. They're never uploaded anywhere by Indium. If the folder is in iCloud Drive or another synced folder, that service syncs it under its own terms.

## Network use

Indium connects to the internet only to load images that a note links to by web address. Those requests go straight to the site hosting the image, which sees your IP address like any web request. Nothing about you or your notes is sent with them.

The direct-download version also checks GitHub for app updates. The Mac App Store version gets updates through the App Store instead.

## Apple Intelligence

If you turn on note icon suggestions, Indium uses Apple Intelligence on your Mac to pick an icon. This runs on your device, and the icons are stored only on your Mac.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/LegendarySpy/Indium/issues.

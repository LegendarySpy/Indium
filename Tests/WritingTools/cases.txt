### Proofreading the selection replaces it, and Undo brings it back
<<< note
Intro line.

«i went to the store and buyed milk eggs and bread.»

Outro line.
<<< propose selection
I went to the store and bought milk, eggs and bread.
>>> note
Intro line.

I went to the store and bought milk, eggs and bread.

Outro line.
>>> undone
Intro line.

i went to the store and buyed milk eggs and bread.

Outro line.
